Our short & sweet story

Two friends, one obsession.

Philip and Alan met at a dinner party and bonded over their love of cheesecake. They started baking at home with a family recipe, and word spread about the new dessert kid in town.

  • First bakery in Notting Hill, after swapping office jobs for the kitchen

  • Cheesecakes for the London 2012 Olympics

How much is delivery?

Nothing extra: we won't charge you for delivery, whatever date you choose. We deliver to most of mainland UK; for anywhere else, email us and we'll look at the options.

When will it arrive?

On the date you pick at checkout, Tuesday to Saturday. In a hurry? Our next-day service works for orders received and confirmed before midday.

How does it arrive?

Vacuum sealed, frozen and packed in a polystyrene box with dry ice, inside a branded DPD box. Make sure someone can take it in, or set your preference with DPD.

How long does it take to defrost?

At least 8 hours. Once defrosted, keep it in the fridge for 2–4 days (check the best-before label) and don't refreeze it. Serve chilled.

How many people will it serve?

6-inch cakes serve 8, 9-inch serve 14, traybakes 15 and minis 9. Most cakes come pre-portioned.

Can I change my order?

Yes, by email within 3 hours of ordering (30 minutes for next-day delivery) at hello@englishcheesecake.com.
