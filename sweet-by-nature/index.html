Our story

It started with one perfect cookie.

In 1990 Megan Misso baked a batch of chocolate chip cookies in her home kitchen for a Fitzroy café. They sold out on the first day. The Genuine Cookie Company became Sweet by Nature, and the tiny Brunswick Street bakehouse became a kitchen in Heidelberg West.

  • Over 100 people in the team today
  • Supplying hundreds of cafés, hospitals, department stores and hotels
  • Show-stoppers for weddings, celebrations and corporate events

How much notice do you need for a cake?

For most cakes, one business day. At busy times of the year we may need more, so order as early as you can; we happily take advance orders.

How many people does each cake serve?

5" serves 6–8, 7" serves 10–12, 9" serves 16–20 and 12" serves 22–28 dessert portions.

Where do you deliver?

All over metro Melbourne, Monday to Saturday between 8am and 5pm, in refrigerated vans. The delivery fee is calculated at checkout from your postcode. For an event, many customers order for the day before.

It’s a gift. Can you add a message?

Yes. Tick “This is a gift” at checkout and we write your message on a postcard at no extra cost. Candles can be added as accessories, and we can leave the price off the delivery.

Do you have gluten free and vegan options?

Yes, we have gluten friendly and vegan friendly ranges in the online shop, and several of our macarons, friands and brookies are gluten free recipes.

Can I visit?

Yes: our café and bakery outlet are at 131 Bamfield Rd, Heidelberg West. We’re closed on Sundays and public holidays.
